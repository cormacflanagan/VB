How to read it

  • A blank row is not a loner. Only 76 of the 1770 possible pairings inside this group have ever happened, because most of these girls partner with someone outside these 60. Kalenna Thiele has no in-group partnership at all, yet has played 7 competitions with Charli Lilley. The second table is where those live.
  • A competition, not a match. Each count is one event-division entered together, the same unit the class reports use, so a weekend played as a pair counts once however many matches it ran to.
  • Doubles only. Club, 3v3 and 5v5 entries are excluded throughout, so these are genuine two-player partnerships.
  • Partner counts include everyone, rated or not, inside this group or outside it. That is the honest denominator for "how settled is she" — Brooke Proctor, Summer Tukua, Ariyah McKinley, Whitney Mertens played the whole year with a single partner.
