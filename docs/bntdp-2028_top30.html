How to read it

  • Each division is its own competition. At the AVP Juniors National Championships the 18U, 17U, 16U and 15U brackets ran as separate fields; they appear as separate columns, and a 5th in one says nothing about a 5th in another.
  • Doubles only — club formats are excluded. The BVCA Orange County dates, Club v Club, the AAU club championships and every 3v3 or 5v5 division are team events where a placing reflects a squad of five to twelve, not the athlete. 79 such results were dropped. The test is roster size rather than the division name, which is unreliable: “Open (5v5)”, “OPEN”, “Club Division” and “Girls Open (5 Pairs)” are all team formats. Every entry kept has exactly one partner.
  • CBVA events carry a link to CBVA. Volleyball Life sanctions the Southern California circuit as "AVPA", so membership is taken from CBVA's own tournament listing, matched on date and venue — an event is tagged only when CBVA itself lists it.
  • Ties are shared. Beach draws award equal finishes to every team knocked out in the same round, which is why blocks of 5th, 9th and 17th recur down a column.
  • Field size is the number of teams registered in that division, as recorded by Volleyball Life — not the number that ultimately played, so a withdrawal leaves the denominator unchanged.
  • Sanctioning bodies are tagged in each column head: AVPA (AVP and AVP Juniors), USAV, AAU, BVCA, CBVA and p1440.
  • How these 30 were chosen. Volleyball Life publishes no class ranking, so the field was built by crawling the partner graph outward from known athletes in the class and keeping every profile that reports a 2028 graduation year. The crawl was then run to closure — repeatedly expanding the partners of every player already found until a full round turned up nobody new above a 7.0 rating floor. That took the cohort to 1,574 girls in the class, 1,381 of them rated; the 30 highest TruVolley scores are the roster here. The cut is tight: #31 is Kate Berry at 7.851, 0.055 behind #30. This is a ranking by rating, not a scouting opinion, and TruVolley rewards win rate, so a light schedule in small fields can flatter a player.
