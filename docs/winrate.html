The same bands over twice the window: 79 matches instead of 35. Wider intervals collapse, and the picture is less flattering — which is what a bigger sample usually does.

How this is built, and what it cannot tell you

  • The axis is the opponent team's mean TruVolley, not the stronger of the two. A 9.2 paired with a 6.0 lands at 7.6, which understates how hard that side is to beat when the strong player takes most of the ball.
  • Only 49 of her 90 matches this year can be placed here. A match needs a published TruVolley for both opponents, and the ones missing it are disproportionately adult players in local draws — so the chart leans toward her junior schedule more than her whole schedule does.
  • Win rate is not the same as rating. A player who enters only events she can win posts a high rate; the interesting number is where the rate breaks, which is what the band structure is for.
  • Empty bands are empty, not zero. Where a band shows a dash she played nobody at that level, which is itself the finding: her schedule has gaps above 8.25.
