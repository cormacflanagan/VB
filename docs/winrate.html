How this is built, and what it cannot tell you

  • The axis is the opponent team's mean TruVolley, not the stronger of the two. A 9.2 paired with a 6.0 lands at 7.6, which understates how hard that side is to beat when the strong player takes most of the ball.
  • Only 83 of her 97 matches this year can be placed here. A match needs a published TruVolley for both opponents, and the ones missing it are disproportionately adult players in local draws — so the chart leans toward her junior schedule more than her whole schedule does.
  • Win rate is not the same as rating. A player who enters only events she can win posts a high rate; the interesting number is where the rate breaks, which is what the band structure is for.
  • Empty bands are empty, not zero. Where a band shows a dash she played nobody at that level, which is itself the finding: her schedule has gaps above 8.25.
  • Open play is CBVA's record, not Volleyball Life's. Volleyball Life files a CBVA event as a finish order and nothing else, so her open results existed here only as placings until the games were crawled from CBVA directly. Both wins over teams rated above her come from that source; neither is visible on her Volleyball Life profile as a match.
