What counts, and what does not

Six kinds of date, and the difference between them is the whole plan. Above her is the number of individual players in that draw rated better than her 9.68 and median is the field's median TruVolley. Sets is what a pair rated like hers actually played, and contested is the part of it against a pair within 0.75 of her own — the sets that were a contest rather than a formality. Contested sets are the currency; everything else is a proxy for them. Days is how long her own draw runs, which is not the same as how long the event runs: AAU Hermosa and AVP Juniors Nationals each occupy five days of the pier, and an age group plays three of them. It is counted from the dates her division actually played last time, so 18U and 17U at the same championship can differ.

Selection

A national-team or ISF trial. The only rows that can put her on a team.

National junior

A national junior championship: the deepest fields she is age-eligible for, and the longest days.

Junior circuit

The p1440 / IBVL / BVNE junior circuit. Regular, shallower, useful for reps and ranking points.

Women's open

An adult draw. Harder opponents than most junior dates, but four sets rather than twenty.

Pro-am open

A promoter's adult open with a purse -- Highline or AVP Next. Harder than CBVA and three times the sets.

Out of reach

Professional or qualification-only. A median above 10.3 and two or three sets before the exit.

Grass

Played on grass, which carries its own TruVolley. Nothing that happens there can move her sand rating.

Three things are deliberately absent. The recruiting showcases — GNBV, the Western World Mega Collegiate Showcase — are off the list because she is committed to Santa Clara, and the field they draw backs that up: the 2026 Western World showcase carried nobody above her at a median of 7.26. The CIFSS Pairs Championship is a CIF Southern Section event and she is at a Central Coast Section school, so it is not open to her. 16U draws are not listed either: she ages out of 16U in 2027, and the 2026 AVP Juniors Nationals 16U field carried nobody above her in any case. And the AVP League qualifiers and NORCECA playoffs appear only as out of reach — medians above 11.3 with most of the field over 10.5, and a median of one and a half matches before the exit.

Two draws in one LA weekend

One flight, one hotel, two draws. It is available more often than the calendar above makes it look, because of how the two circuits schedule around each other: CBVA has run two women’s opens in the LA basin on consecutive days exactly once in three seasons, but the junior circuit deliberately takes the day CBVA is not using. Highline, AVP Next, BRAVO and Mexico

Four names worth checking, and they do not turn out to be four options. Two are real and sit a tier above anything else she can drive to; one is a good series in the wrong state; and the Mexican one is a tier she cannot enter, under a name that stopped existing in 2022.

The pro-am circuit in California

Highline is a Los Angeles promoter running one- and two-day adult opens with purses from $2,500 to $10,000 — Dockweiler, Ocean Park, Manhattan, Venice, Huntington, and away dates at Santa Barbara and Coronado.   • Against a field with a median of 7.67 she is an 86% favourite in a typical match. That figure is not a guess: across 7,000 rated CBVA women's matches the stronger pair's win rate tracks a logistic in the rating gap almost exactly — 57% at a half-point gap, 81% at a point and a half, 91% at two and a half. A match she wins seven times in eight carries almost no information and almost no rating.
  • So: play it, and count it as paid practice. The only rule is that it never substitutes for a travel date — and on the 2027 calendar above, the Santa Cruz slots fall in months that already hold something better.
  • The prize is not a reason by itself. At roughly $200 for first it is a good Saturday, not a revenue stream, and it is the one number on this page that comes from you rather than from the data.

The NTDP series

Four invitational residential camps a year, not competitions — no draw and no result — but they are the pool the January trials are selected from, and she has been on five consecutive rosters. The winter series runs on the following year's bands, so the Winter 2026 camp is her first as a U18 rather than a U17, and it lands immediately before the January trials. USA Volleyball dates them a few months out; the 2026 winter series is still undated.

How to read it, and what it is not

  • An estimated date is an estimate. Half of these rows are projected from a series that has run in the same week for two or three years. Organisers move dates; confirm before booking anything.
  • The field numbers describe last year's draw, not next year's. They are the best guide available to what a date is worth, but the 2027 entry list does not exist yet, and a selection event's field in particular turns over every season.
  • Ratings are current TruVolley, applied to fields up to two years old, so an older edition reads slightly stronger than it was. Unrated entrants are excluded from the medians, which skews them a little high.
  • The day count is last year's, not next year's. It is the number of distinct dates her division played at the most recent edition, so it catches the part of a five-day festival that belongs to her age group — but a tour that adds a day or merges two age groups changes it, and the 2027 listings mostly post a single opening date rather than a span.
  • Age bands are birth-year ceilings. U-N in year Y admits anyone born in Y−N+1 or later, so an 18U draw in 2027 admits 2010 and younger. She is the oldest year in it. In 2028 she ages out of U18 and that year's U19 becomes the equivalent position.
  • Trials entry is not the same as trials eligibility. Nineteen of the twenty-four entrants at the 2026 U18 trials came off the NTDP rosters she was also on, and athletes two years younger than her entered, so the route is open — but the published criteria should be confirmed with her NTDP contact rather than inferred from who turned up.
  • Nevada contributes two dates and neither is a reason to go. Arizona earns its place because the p1440 circuit runs Phoenix dates she can reach; Nevada is listed for completeness, and what the record holds is a December girls' pairs date in Las Vegas and a Tahoe Saturday played on grass, which carries its own rating and cannot move her sand one. Out-of-region national events — the Futures Tour stops in Florida, Texas and Washington — are left out because they are not a day's travel.
