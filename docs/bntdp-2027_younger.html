How to read it

  • Each division is its own competition. At the AVP Juniors National Championships the 18U, 17U, 16U and 15U brackets ran as separate fields; they appear as separate columns, and a 5th in one says nothing about a 5th in another.
  • Doubles only — club formats are excluded. The BVCA Orange County dates, Club v Club, the AAU club championships and every 3v3 or 5v5 division are team events where a placing reflects a squad of five to twelve, not the athlete. 201 such results were dropped. The test is roster size rather than the division name, which is unreliable: “Open (5v5)”, “OPEN”, “Club Division” and “Girls Open (5 Pairs)” are all team formats. Every entry kept has exactly one partner.
  • CBVA events carry a link to CBVA. Volleyball Life sanctions the Southern California circuit as "AVPA", so membership is taken from CBVA's own tournament listing, matched on date and venue — an event is tagged only when CBVA itself lists it.
  • Ties are shared. Beach draws award equal finishes to every team knocked out in the same round, which is why blocks of 5th, 9th and 17th recur down a column.
  • Field size is the number of teams registered in that division, as recorded by Volleyball Life — not the number that ultimately played, so a withdrawal leaves the denominator unchanged.
  • Sanctioning bodies are tagged in each column head: AVPA (AVP and AVP Juniors), USAV, AAU, BVCA, CBVA and p1440.
  • Why this is not a graduating class. A bracket is age-eligible, not single-year: an 18U field is grad 2027 and younger, so the girls a 2027 player actually meets include every strong 2028 or 2029 playing up. This group is built on that rule — the partner graph crawled to closure on gradYear >= 2027 — giving 17,178 girls, 14,398 of them rated, of whom the 60 highest TruVolley scores are the roster here. The cut is tight: #61 is Taylie Hansen at 8.072, 0.030 behind #60. It is a ranking by rating, not a scouting opinion, and TruVolley rewards win rate, so a light schedule in small fields can flatter a player.
