Where the 17U draw actually exists

No turnout bar is applied here; a 17U draw existing is the fact worth knowing.

How to read it, and what it is not

  • Turnout counts athletes, not teams. A number is how many of that tier entered that bracket; a girl who played both the 18U and the 17U is counted in both rows, which is the point of splitting them.
  • Brackets are folded onto the age they are actually played at. Organisers label one field a dozen ways — "Girls 18U", "U18 Girls", "Girls 18:U (Grad Year 2026-2027)" and "Class of '26 & Younger" are the same competition. An explicit age wins; failing that the youngest graduating year admitted sets the ceiling. The raw division names sit under each bracket label, with the count beside them. Women's is the adult open draw, which several of this class enter and which is a materially harder field than the juniors bracket at the same event.
  • Doubles only. Club, 3v3 and 5v5 entries are excluded throughout, on the same rule the class reports use — a placing in those says little about an individual.
  • This is a map of the class of 2027, who are a year ahead of the class of 2028. At a grad-year event an athlete from the younger class enters a different division, so what transfers is where the strong fields gather, not the bracket itself. The three columns tell you how deep the older class's field was, which is the part worth chasing.
  • An empty last column mostly means "not posted yet", not "not happening". Only 5 of these 70 events have a 2026–2027 edition listed so far. AVP Juniors Nationals, BVCA Pairs Nationals and the Futures Tour stops are typically published a few months out, so they will appear closer to the date. Matching is by name after stripping years and ordinals, so a renamed event will also miss.
  • CBVA events link out to CBVA. CBVA runs much of the Southern California circuit, but Volleyball Life records the sanction as "AVPA", so the name and the sanction field cannot tell you which events are theirs. The tag is set by matching our events against CBVA's own tournament listing on date and venue, so an event carries it only when CBVA lists it; the link goes to that event's CBVA page, and to the specific division where the two agree on its name.
  • The training series are not tournaments. They are invitational residential camps with no draw and no result, so Volleyball Life has no record of them; dates and rosters come from USA Volleyball, and each row links to the announcement they were read from. Their turnout columns are a count of selections, not entries, and they are not comparable with a tournament's: a camp roster is a dozen or two by design. The age groups vary by series — the spring series ran no U18 group at all, because the Girls U18 National Team trained alongside it instead. The winter series for 2026–2027 has not been dated yet.
  • An invitation is not a result, but it repeats. Across the four series 101 girls were invited at least once and 6 to all four. Of the 33 girls on the two summer rosters this repo tracks, most had been to two or three of the earlier camps — so the summer roster reads as the end of a year's selection rather than a single decision.
  • Locations come from the tournament record and are blank where the organiser left them unset, which is common for one-day local events.
