Four games, unless you win

Every CBVA open runs the same shape — a pool, then a playoff — and the number of matches that produces barely moves with the size of the draw. An eight-team event and a forty-team event both hand a typical team four. Six or seven means a top-three finish, and that is the only lever there is.

January to April

CBVA has never run a women’s open in January or February — not once, in a database going back to 2005. Its season opens in the third week of March, and the March–April draws are the weakest of the year. Two winter dates exist outside CBVA, both at the top of the sport: the BNTDP Jr Pan Am Games Trials in January, which is an invitation, and Highline’s $5K Sponsorship Open at Ocean Park North on the last Saturday of February — 17 teams to a median of 10.01, nine of them above 10.5.

Her pair rating is what the comparison runs on, not her own: she entered the 2026 opens with partners at 9.49 and 9.13, so a team averaging above 9.50 is a team that outranks hers on paper. Change the partner and every “teams above her” number on this page moves.

How to read it, and what it is not

  • Ratings are current, the events are not. Every entrant carries the TruVolley she holds today, applied to fields up to three years old, so an older draw reads a little stronger than it was. It says who turned out to be good, not who looked good on the day.
  • Unrated entrants are left out of the medians. They skew weak — inactive or unprofiled players — so every median here is slightly optimistic. At the thin venues (North Beach, Belmont Shore, Newland) only one or two pairs per draw are fully rated, so a zero in the “teams above her” column is a real signal but a soft one.
  • Games are counted from the published pools and brackets, so a team that forfeited or never checked in shows no matches and is dropped from the count rather than recorded as zero.
  • Doubles only, and sand only. Grass carries a separate TruVolley, as does coed and as do triples and quads: a player with a hundred grass or coed matches and no sand doubles has a sand rating of zero. None of those results can move the number this page ranks on.
  • A draw existing is not a draw running. Fields are listed as entered; CBVA cancels or merges small divisions, and the capacity published weeks out is not the field that shows up.
