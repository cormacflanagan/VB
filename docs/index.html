Fifteen reports on who actually plays whom

Every page here is built from Volleyball Life results for junior girls doubles. They answer the questions a ranking number cannot: which events a cohort really turns up to, who partners with whom, and how much of a rating gap is signal rather than a thin record.

Record by competition

The core report. For each athlete in a roster or cut: her TruVolley, her competitions, her finishes, and the strength of the field she beat to get them — so a result at a 60-team open is not read the same way as one at an eight-team local. Each carries a head-to-head crosstable of who has met whom inside the group.

Partnerships

Beach is a two-player game, so a result belongs to a pair rather than a player. These pages ask how settled each athlete is: how many different partners she has used in a year, and which pairings recur. A matrix covers partnerships inside the group; a second table catches the partners from outside it, which the matrix structurally cannot show.

Ratings, and how far to trust them

TruVolley is Volleyball Life's own number. These two pages interrogate it rather than report it.

The 2027 field, re-ranked

A Bradley-Terry rating fitted to 679,241 matches from Volleyball Life, CBVA and college beach, over a closed cohort of 17,119 girls — then 40 bootstrap refits, resampled by tournament-division, to put an interval on every place. The point is that most of the order is not separable: a rank built on a thin record moves a long way when you refit it.

17,119 girls · 679,241 matches · 40 refits

Fitted, not TruVolley

Where her wins stop

One athlete against opponent strength in quarter-point TruVolley bands — the same increment the rating is quoted in — with 95% Wilson intervals, to find the level at which winning stops. Now carries every match against a team rated above her, including the Women's Open playoff wins that only CBVA records.

How to read these

  • A competition, not a match. Counts are event-divisions entered, so a weekend played as a pair counts once however many matches it ran to.
  • Doubles only. Club, 3v3 and 5v5 entries are excluded throughout, so every partnership on these pages is a genuine two-player one.
  • A cut is a snapshot. "Top 60" means top 60 on the TruVolley standing at the retrieval date. Volleyball Life replaced the TruVolley formula in September 2026 — ranks on these pages are not comparable with ranks printed before that.
  • The head-to-head crosstables are older than the ratings beside them. On the record-by-competition pages the crosstable was pulled 20 August 2026; the ratings and cuts are 14 September 2026. Each page names the gap and how many of its athletes entered the cut after the snapshot. The partnership pages are not affected.
